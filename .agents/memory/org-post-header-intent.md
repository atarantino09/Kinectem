---
name: Organization post header intent
description: Shared branding and identity hierarchy for posts associated with an organization.
---

Organization branding is not recap-exclusive. All org-associated post types should use the same single branded identity header, not an additional organization banner above the original header.

**Why:** The user rejected the duplicated organization banner and clarified that the new brand colors belong on the original header. The team name must remain larger than the organization name, with a smaller author line.

**How to apply:** Preserve this hierarchy when changing post cards or introducing new post types, including compact featured recap cards on competition pages. Those cards must match the org header branding used elsewhere, not use a lighter substitute palette. Use the actual owning organization’s identity and saved palette; keep competition identity separate. Styling must not expand which posts are allowed on an organization page or change private-post permissions.
