---
name: Visible team and organization follow controls
description: Keep follow/unfollow and the follower count together and directly visible in page headers.
---

Team and organization pages must have one directly visible follow/unfollow
button with the follower total included. Do not hide following in a Community
menu or require a separate follower-count button.

Use the visible labels **Follow** and **Following**, not **Unfollow**.
Clicking **Following** still removes the follow.

**Why:** The user wants following to be obvious and available without opening a
menu, including when looking at a team they manage. They explicitly requested
Following as the active-state label rather than Unfollow.

**How to apply:** Preserve this single control during header redesigns and
mobile layout changes. Its action and count must reflect the current follow
state, and repeated clicks must be disabled while the request is pending.
