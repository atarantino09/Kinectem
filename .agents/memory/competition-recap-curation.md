---
name: Competition recap curation
description: Product boundary between coach publication, organizer review and personal recap mail.
---

Competition review is curation, not permission to publish a coach's team post.
Acknowledging without featuring must leave the team's published recap intact;
removing a featured recap must not restore outstanding review.

**Why:** Organizers need to clear their review queue without taking down a
coach's work. Organization publication approval is a separate workflow and must
not be coupled to competition curation.

**How to apply:** Keep review decisions separate from article status and from
notification read state. Never repurpose review actions to delete or unpublish
team posts.

Changing personal recap delivery modes discards unsent backlog rather than
replaying it under the new mode. Opting out affects mail only.

**Why:** Busy competitions can generate many recaps; resuming email must not
produce a historical flood or hide unresolved reviews.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between personal delivery choices,
in-app alerts and shared outstanding curation.
