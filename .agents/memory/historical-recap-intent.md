---
name: Historical recap intent
description: Product boundaries for digitizing schools' newspaper history.
---

Historical recaps are faithful transcriptions of original newspaper articles, not AI-written replacements. The author reviews the transcript, including unreadable passages.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose original wording so schools can share past games with alumni, without inventing details or treating old players as today's roster.

**How to apply:** Keep archival date/year and source attribution independent of current-game controls. Do not infer current roster tags, competitions, or schedule completion from archival dates or names. A school can publish its history directly without a current team or a placeholder team. This does not open publishing or external-AI access to ordinary alumni or athletes.

Private newspaper originals are not ordinary reusable media: copying their URLs into another post must never change who can read them.

**Why:** An unrelated published cover reference could otherwise expose a private draft's clipping or revive a moderated original.

**How to apply:** Future media attachment paths must preserve the clipping's historical authoring and moderation boundary. Public access needs a validated, visible historical publication, not simply any published post containing the same URL.

Historical recap creation belongs in the organization's **New post** choices and the team's **Other post options** dropdown, not separate standalone page buttons.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these locations so historical recaps are discoverable with the existing post types.

**How to apply:** Preserve organization-manager and team-recap-author permissions and pass the current organization or team into the historical composer.
