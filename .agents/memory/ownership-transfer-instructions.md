---
name: Claimed organization ownership handoff
description: Explain the owner-controlled handoff as invitation, acceptance, then a separate confirmed transfer
---

For an already-claimed organization, make the current owner's handoff steps
explicit: invite the intended successor using **Add admin**, wait for acceptance,
then use **Transfer ownership** beside their name and confirm. Someone already
listed as a member or admin can skip the invitation steps.

**Why:** The user confirmed that merely saying "only the owner can transfer
ownership" was insufficient. They want owners to understand that an accepted
admin invitation does not itself make its recipient the owner.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction in future handoff wording and show
the instructions to the current owner, not ordinary admins. Explain that the
previous owner remains an admin. This applies to claimed-org transfers, not
the platform admin's separate owner-invitation flow for an unclaimed org.
