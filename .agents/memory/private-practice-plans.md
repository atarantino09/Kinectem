---
name: Private practice plans
description: Privacy and reuse rules for internal coach updates built on article infrastructure.
---

Practice Plans are an explicit private article kind, not public recaps hidden only by the client. They reuse durable article drafts and AI authoring, but must be excluded from every public feed, profile, organization, search, newsletter, and follower surface.

**Why:** These updates can contain team-only coaching information intended for accepted players, their active guardians, team staff, and organization owners/admins. A copied URL or secondary query must not reveal the plan to followers, fans, unrelated teams, or anonymous visitors.

**How to apply:** Enforce access on the server for list and detail reads; return 404 to unauthorized readers. Keep drafts staff-only, scope AI Assist to the selected team, and treat new article query surfaces as public until they explicitly exclude Practice Plans.
