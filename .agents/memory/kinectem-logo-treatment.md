---
name: Kinectem logo treatment
description: User-approved constraints for applying the Kinectem logo correction in the web app and marketing site.
---

Use the supplied transparent horizontal lockup for inline marks on dark
surfaces. Keep a contrast-appropriate light-surface counterpart for white
chrome, using the same corrected geometry. The stacked transparent lockup is
only for a layout that is already designed for a stacked mark.

Do not use the existing stacked-light raster in marketing footers. At compact
footer sizes its geometry reads as a separated "K" and "em," even when enlarged.
Use the transparent white horizontal lockup there.

**Why:** The requested change was limited to raising the K into better
alignment with the wordmark. A broader brand-system replacement, opaque navy
logo treatment, altered scale, and the broken-looking compact stacked footer
mark were explicitly rejected.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing logo dimensions, surrounding layouts,
and page colors. Make asset-only changes unless a new, intentional stacked
placement is introduced.

For Kinectem-owned chrome, broad gradients run at 135 degrees from purple to
blue. Magenta is limited to leading glyph or wordmark accents, not broad
surfaces. Organization, team, competition, and user branding remains
authoritative: never tint, blend, dim, or place Kinectem overlays over uploaded
logos, banners, custom palettes, or cover photos.

**Why:** The approved 2026 app refresh extends the marketing language into
neutral chrome without replacing the identity users bring to their pages.

**How to apply:** Use the Kinectem gradient for neutral hierarchy and calls to
action. When custom media or colors exist, show them directly and keep any
Kinectem treatment outside their bounds.

The supplied 1200×630 light image is a social sharing card. Serve it unchanged
and reference it from Open Graph/Twitter metadata; do not scale or crop it into
the header lockup.
