---
name: Partner suggestions and identity
description: Why schedule-assignment suggestions are advisory name evidence rather than identity or gender decisions.
---

Partner-directory schedule suggestions must remain advisory name evidence. Duplicate team names can be legitimate separate identities, including teams in distinct clubs or with different genders. Do not resolve ambiguity by treating gender metadata as an automatic assignment decision.

**Why:** The product requires a platform admin to choose the destination and retain the existing name-warning review and batch confirmation. Suggestions must not create ownership, alter ordinary claim approvals, or introduce a different gender-warning policy.

**How to apply:** Future matching improvements may improve ordering and readable metadata, but must preserve explicit selection and review and show duplicate identities separately.
