---
name: Parent roster removal
description: Parents need simple child-specific controls in both team roster views without deleting a child account
---

Parents should have a visible pencil beside their own child in both the team
page's side roster and its full Roster list, including mobile. Either active
parent/guardian should be able to remove their child from that team.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested that leaving a team be very easy and
simple for parents in both places. Leaving one team must not delete the child's
account, other team memberships, or family relationships.

**How to apply:** Keep one clear removal confirmation for non-manager parents,
not a staff-role editor. Preserve existing coach/org-admin controls, and don't
limit parent controls to the primary guardian.
