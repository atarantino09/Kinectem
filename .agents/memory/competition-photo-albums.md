---
name: Competition photo album scope
description: Product rules for competition contributions, moderation, and subtle branding across team pages
---

Competition photos are one gallery per competition in this version, not separately named albums. Competition admins upload directly; participating-team community contributions are optional and require approval. Keep contribution settings off initially. A contributor must clearly see that submission does not mean publication.

**Why:** The user requested bulk organizer uploads, optional team submissions, easy bulk review, and approved photos shared across multiple participating team pages.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between actual competition administrative membership and invisible platform-support review access. Support access alone must not grant uploading or make support users recipients of contribution alerts. Keep competition moderation separate from existing recap-album behavior.

Use small competition branding on gallery tiles and full-screen viewers, including appearances on tagged team pages. Prefer a discreet corner logo, with a compact competition-name fallback; do not permanently bake the overlay into original files.

**Why:** The user's additional direction was “keep some competition branding on the photos as well” and “small and subtle.” The approved scope preserves original uploads while keeping their competition attribution on every app surface.

**How to apply:** New photo surfaces must retain the competition attribution without large panels or obscuring the photo. Multiple team appearances are references to a single moderated source, not copies with independent approval state.

The competition-page Photo Album section should start collapsed on ordinary visits. Keep a thorough, administrator-only explanation inside the photos section covering direct uploads, eligibility, private submissions, batch notifications, moderation, multi-team distribution, and removal. Explicit album/review links may expand it to reach their destination.

**Why:** The user requested a collapsed initial section and a “better more thorough explaination” for the competition admin about exactly how the feature works. The album should not dominate the initial page, but organizer instructions must remain available where the work happens.

**How to apply:** Preserve the collapsed default when adding controls or guidance. Do not hide the destination when an admin follows a pending-review alert.
