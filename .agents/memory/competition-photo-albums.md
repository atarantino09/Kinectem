---
name: Competition photo album scope
description: Product rules for competition contributions, moderation, and subtle branding across team pages
---

Competition photos are one gallery per competition in this version, not separately named albums. Competition admins upload directly; participating-team community contributions default ON but still require approval. Administrators may turn contributions off. A contributor must clearly see that submission does not mean publication.

**Why:** The user requested bulk organizer uploads, easy bulk review, and approved photos shared across multiple participating team pages. They subsequently changed the contribution policy to default ON: approval already prevents unreviewed photos from appearing publicly. They explicitly chose to enable existing competitions as well, including formerly disabled ones, once; later admin opt-outs must remain respected.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between actual competition administrative membership and invisible platform-support review access. Support access alone must not grant uploading or make support users recipients of contribution alerts. Keep competition moderation separate from existing recap-album behavior.

Use small competition branding on gallery tiles and full-screen viewers, including appearances on tagged team pages. Prefer a discreet corner logo, with a compact competition-name fallback; do not permanently bake the overlay into original files.

**Why:** The user's additional direction was “keep some competition branding on the photos as well” and “small and subtle.” The approved scope preserves original uploads while keeping their competition attribution on every app surface.

**How to apply:** New photo surfaces must retain the competition attribution without large panels or obscuring the photo. Multiple team appearances are references to a single moderated source, not copies with independent approval state.

The competition-page Photo Album section should start collapsed on ordinary visits. Keep a thorough, administrator-only explanation inside the photos section covering direct uploads, eligibility, private submissions, batch notifications, moderation, multi-team distribution, and removal. Explicit album/review links may expand it to reach their destination.

**Why:** The user requested a collapsed initial section and a “better more thorough explaination” for the competition admin about exactly how the feature works. The album should not dominate the initial page, but organizer instructions must remain available where the work happens.

**How to apply:** Preserve the collapsed default when adding controls or guidance, including the Photo album panel in the competition editor. Keep its title and photo counts visible while collapsed. Do not hide the destination when an admin follows a pending-review alert.

Competition albums, including approved photos, are signed-in-only. Visitors must see “Sign in to see photo albums” with a sign-in link rather than photo previews.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires visitors to sign in before seeing competition photo albums. Approval permits viewing by signed-in users; it does not make photo files publicly accessible.

**How to apply:** Enforce authentication on album lists, direct photo streams, and tagged-team appearances, not only the competition-page UI. Keep private/no-store responses and remove gallery/viewer state on sign-out. Do not change ordinary post-album permissions.

Ordinary signed-in viewers should receive a short album explanation and be able to download approved photos, without needing upload, review, or administrative permissions.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested instructions explaining the album and contribution/approval process to non-admins, and a clearly advertised way for signed-in viewers to save photos.

**How to apply:** Keep brief member-facing guidance inside the expanded album alongside the separate thorough organizer guide. Explain contribution eligibility, approval, private submission status, and how to download an opened photo. Download through the authenticated photo stream; do not make pending/rejected submissions public or create public storage links.
