---
name: Combined recap chronology
description: Coaches batch-write recaps after games; competition dates, not authoring dates, must ground the combined story.
---

Coaches may write all their weekend game recaps on Monday. Writing or publication dates do not establish when matches happened or whether multiple games were played on one day.

**Why:** The user described two Saturday and two Sunday games being narrated incorrectly as four games in a single day.

**How to apply:** Ground combined recaps in linked competition schedule dates where available. Otherwise use explicitly known game dates, not authoring timestamps; avoid unsupported phrases such as “our third game of the day” when chronology is unknown. Coaches should be able to select a competition’s team matches together rather than manually identifying separately posted recaps.
