---
name: Team competition schedule display
description: Product scope for the simplified competition game cards on team pages.
---

On team pages, ordinary competition game details should be limited to opponent, date, and bracket.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested only these three details in this section, rather than the fuller imported schedule.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce time, venue, field, state, division, or home/away prefixes when revising these cards. This is a team-page presentation rule, not a request to delete imported schedule data or simplify other schedule screens. Preserve cancellation warnings and authorized management/recap actions.
