---
name: Organization sport inheritance
description: Rules for organization-wide versus per-team sport labels.
---

A nonblank organization sport is authoritative: it labels every existing team, is inherited by new teams, and cannot be overridden from an individual team page. A blank organization sport means teams choose their sports independently.

**Why:** Single-sport clubs need one consistent label everywhere, while schools and other multi-sport organizations must be able to assign different sports team by team.

**How to apply:** When an organization selects or changes its sport, propagate it to all teams. When the organization clears its sport, do not erase existing team labels; simply restore team-level editing.
