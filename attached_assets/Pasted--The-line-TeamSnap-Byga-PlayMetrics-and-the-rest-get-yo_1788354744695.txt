**Main block:**

**You already have software that runs your season.**

TeamSnap, Byga, PlayMetrics, GotSport, Stack — whichever one your club is on, it handles registration, rosters, schedules, payments, and who's bringing the oranges. It gets you to kickoff. That's what it's built for, and none of that changes when you add Kinectem.

But none of those platforms were built to hold onto a season once it's over. Rosters get rebuilt every August. Group chats scroll away. The season ends and the team quietly disappears from the app. Ten years of a kid's soccer life leaves behind a shoebox of medals and a camera roll nobody can search.

**Kinectem starts where those platforms stop.**

After every game, your coach publishes a game recap — and writing one takes a couple of minutes, not an evening. The coach jots down a few notes: the score, a couple of key moments, the player who came off the bench with energy and sparked the comeback, the work they've been putting in at practice finally showing up on the field. The plays that never make a box score but were the real story of the game. AI Assist turns those notes into a full game recap. The coach reads it over, makes it sound like them, and publishes.

One button auto-tags every rostered player. That game recap lands on the team page and on every one of those players' pages, and it stays there permanently. Do that for a season and the team page holds the whole season in order, start to finish, and every player on your roster has a written record of their own journey. Do it for a few years and a kid graduating has hundreds of games written down by the coaches who were there.

**And your club page is where all of it comes together.**

Every club on Kinectem gets one public club page, and it fills itself in. Every team, every game recap, every player, under your crest, updating on its own as the weekend happens.

Nobody has to build it or maintain it. A coach jots down a few notes at the final whistle, and before anyone's out of the parking lot, families from every other team in the club know exactly how that game went. The U9 game and the U17 game sit right next to each other on the same page, and nobody had to go looking for either one. For the first time your club looks like one program instead of a dozen separate group chats that happen to share a logo.

It's also the page you send a family who's deciding between your club and the one in the next town over. Not a schedule and a fee table — a living record of what actually happens at your club every single weekend, written by your own coaches.
