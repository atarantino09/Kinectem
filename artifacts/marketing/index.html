Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting your organization set up on Kinectem.

How hard is it to set up? +
Start with your organization and teams, then invite staff and families. Guided setup keeps each step clear, and you can begin publishing without replacing the tools you already use.
Is my kid actually safe on this platform? +
Yes. Kinectem was built from the ground up to be fully COPPA-compliant. Parents have complete control over their child's profile visibility, who can contact them, and what information is shared publicly.
I'm a coach — I don't have time to write a 500-word game recap. +
You don't have to. Add rough game notes, let AI Assist create a polished draft, then review and publish when it sounds right.
Do college coaches actually look at platforms like this? +
A well-organized player storybook gives coaches credible context they can review and share. Kinectem helps athletes collect that record over time; it does not promise recruiting outcomes.
Why not just keep using our club website and Facebook? +
Keep using them. Kinectem adds the structured player, team, and season archive those channels don't create, while giving you stories worth sharing back to them.
Does Kinectem only work for top travel teams? +
No. Kinectem is built for youth sports organizations of every size and level because every athlete deserves a record of the work and moments that shaped their season.
What happens to all the data Kinectem captures about my kid? +
Guardian controls and under-13 protections govern what is collected and visible. Parents can manage their child's profile visibility and the information shared publicly.
